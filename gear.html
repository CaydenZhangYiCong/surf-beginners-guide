Essential gear

Surf gear for beginners

It doesn’t take a huge setup to get started. A few quality basics make learning much easier and safer.

1

Soft-top surfboard

For beginners, a soft-top board is forgiving, stable, and easier to paddle. It’s the best choice for learning the basics.

2

Leash

A leash keeps your board attached to you. This is essential for safety and helps prevent your board from drifting away.

3

Wetsuit

Choose a wetsuit based on water temperature. Even in warm water, a rash guard or light suit can help protect your skin.

What to choose first

A beginner usually needs a soft-top longboard or funboard, a leash, wax, and maybe a rash guard or light wetsuit. Avoid buying a very tiny shortboard until your technique and confidence improve.

Beginner setup

  • Soft-top board
  • Appropriate leash
  • Surf wax
  • Light wetsuit or rash guard

Board length matters

Longer boards are easier to paddle and more forgiving when catching waves. They make it easier to stand up and maintain balance, especially when learning. A fatter, broader board often feels more stable than a narrow one.

Quick rule

If you are new, choose bigger, softer, and more stable over smaller and more technical.

Gear checklist

Before you paddle out

Surfboard with leash
Wax for traction
Wetsuit or rash guard
Water bottle and sunscreen
Towel and dry clothes
Helmet only if needed for local conditions
